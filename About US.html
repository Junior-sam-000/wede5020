ABOUT US

Welcome to Kota Joe, your home of delicious and freshly prepared South African kota meals!

Kota Joe is a fast-food business that focuses on serving tasty, affordable and satisfying meals. We specialise in traditional South African kota filled with delicious ingredients and amazing flavours.

Our goal is to bring people together through good food and excellent customer service. We believe every customer deserves a fresh, enjoyable and memorable food experience.

Our Mission

To provide our customers with affordable, high-quality and delicious kota meals while delivering excellent customer service.

Our Vision

To become one of South Africa's most recognised and trusted kota food brands.

Our Values

Quality, freshness, affordability, customer satisfaction and passion for South African food.
