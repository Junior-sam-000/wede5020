About Kota Joe

Kota Joe started in 2021 in modderforntein with one goal: to serve the best and most filling in sandton. start as a small spaza stand that has grown into a community favorite known for our massive portions,fresh ingredients and that secret Joe sauce everyone talk about. Home of the legendary half,quater and full kota. Made fresh daily.

Our Mission

To bring people together over great kota. We also believe a good kota can can fix a bad day. we're committed to using locally sourced bread and ingredients to support our community.

Our vision

To become South African most loved kota brand - one bite at a time. We want every customer to leave full,happy and come back for more.
